Nuclear Frog

Experimental • RGB • Multiplatform

A 1x1 pixel game that takes advantage of RGB fixed pixel technology to make an entire ecosystem out of just 3 subpixels! You play as the titular Nuclear Frog, trying to irradiate to your full potential, but watch out for the hungry Red Fox! Dive into the Blue Pond to escape! Can you avoid the Red Fox and become the most radioactive Nuclear Frog?
